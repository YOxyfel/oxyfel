03Created by Yane Zhekov

A person first. Clear boundaries.

Eldervice brings my work with software into a practical, local project: helping people feel more confident with the technology they already have.

The Varna pilot focuses on everyday use, setup, and learning together. It is not a hardware repair, medical, or financial service. Tasks and any visit are agreed personally in advance; availability is not guaranteed here.

You enter your own passwords. Device pictures for a personal guide are taken only with your permission. Information is shared with relatives only with your consent.
