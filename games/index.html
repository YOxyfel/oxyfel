Inside Wuhen & Xuan

A world worth defying.

Wuhen & Xuan is a dark xianxia action RPG about cultivation, survival, and the cost of reaching beyond your limits. Its world is taking shape, with a PC demo in development.

An idea becomes a design decision

Let the world be vast.
Keep the first journey specific.

The world document limits the demo footprint to four mortal areas. Capitals, distant sects, and higher realms can be seen or spoken of, while the first journey concentrates on survival and cultivation below.

Authored development scope, not a statement that these areas are finished or publicly playable.

The World, Survival & Territory document names four demo areas: Cinderfall, mountain passes, oceanic cliffs, and forbidden ruins. Other mortal locations and higher realms are vista/lore references within that scope.

Cinderfall — ambient Qi 1.0
The village is the starting anchor for the mortal-world slice. The authored world-systems target is Cinderfall Village; surrounding areas define the intended demo footprint.
Mountain passes — ambient Qi 1.2
One of the four mortal areas in the planned demo scope. In the design notes its ambient Qi is richer than Cinderfall, connecting exploration with the pace of cultivation.
Oceanic cliffs — ambient Qi 1.1
A distinct mortal area in the demo plan. The world document separates places the player is meant to traverse from distant locations that serve as lore or vistas.
Forbidden ruins — ambient Qi 1.5
The only zone in these tunable starting values that meets the 1.5 breakthrough-density threshold. The design gives exploration a concrete purpose: find land rich enough to cultivate further.

The intended loop is to explore, find a spirit vein, establish an abode, and use formations to support cultivation. Implementation and broader-world questions remain open; the PC demo is in development.

Inside Mosquittoz

Perspective changes everything.

Mosquittoz turns a familiar room into an asymmetric multiplayer arena. Humans hunt the nests; mosquitoes feed, bank blood, and grow their colony. The current Windows LAN alpha is built around short rounds in the Safehouse.

Inside the development build

Two roles. Two sets of decisions.

Inspect real character tests, then try the logic behind the swarm. These July 2026 captures show the development test scene, not a finished Safehouse match.

A target you can read.

The Human hunts nests and responds to nearby Mosquittos. This imported character is shown inside the Unreal development test scene.

See all character tests and the real Swarm loop

A successful feed adds one carried unit. Enter a living nest’s deposit volume to bank it. Deposits incubate for six seconds; mature hatchlings support player respawns before surplus AI. Destroying a nest also destroys its incubating and ready brood.

Inside THRESHOLD

Find your limit. Then move it.

THRESHOLD is an adaptive Roblox parkour prototype. A thirteen-section run responds to your performance as you choose when to push and when to recover. Momentum Horizon 0.3.1 is the current local Roblox prototype; the course and its earned-Momentum rules are taking shape through playtesting.

Inspect the mechanic

Momentum comes from what you do.

This small rule example begins with 40 Momentum at the start of an adaptive section. Advance its events, choose a gate, and see what changes. It is an explanation of the game’s rules, not a playable parkour course.

Earned Momentum40/100
Flow inactive
  1. 01Cross progress plane 1+10
  2. 02Cross progress plane 2+10
  3. 03Cross progress plane 3+10
  4. 04Clear the section cleanly+20

0 of 4 section events completed. Each progress plane earns 10 Momentum.

Read the rule example without playing it

Starting at 40, three progress planes earn 10 each: 50 → 60 → 70. A clean first-attempt clear earns 20: 90. Choosing the recommended Hold gate earns 10: 100. Flow activates only upon entering an active adaptive/finale section. Choosing Recover or Push changes the next rating and score factor, but does not earn this example’s recommended-gate bonus.

The real game has thirteen sections and three gates, after sections 5, 8, and 11. Its difficulty classifier recommends a gate based on performance; the player retains the choice. This isolated example omits earlier sections and does not reconstruct the full run.

Based on the current 0.3.1 configuration and Momentum Horizon gameplay contract. The screenshot shows calibration, not a captured Flow activation.

Inside Run & Bank

The expedition is only half the story.

Run & Bank is an early Roblox prototype combining temple parkour, carried loot, and banking. Valuable finds create a decision: press deeper into the route, or secure what you have before a failed run costs it.
