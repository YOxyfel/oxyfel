Privacy.

A simple website, with a clear account of how information is handled.

Last updated: 6 September 2026

01 / This website

The Oxyfel website has no visitor accounts, analytics, or tracking scripts. It does not set cookies for analytics or advertising. Fonts and project imagery are served from the website itself. Interactive examples and guide previews run in your browser.

02 / Hosting and external links

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive technical information, including your IP address, when serving pages and assets. Its handling of that information is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Project pages, GitHub, and the founder's portfolio are separate websites. Following those links takes you to services with their own privacy practices.

03 / If you email us

The inquiry tool prepares an email draft in your browser. Your text is not submitted to a server by this website and is not saved when you leave the page. Opening a draft passes the text to your email application; copying it places it on your clipboard. Guide samples can also be downloaded or printed on your device. If you choose to send a message, we receive the information you include, such as your email address and project details, so we can reply. Your email provider and ours also handle the message.

To ask about information you have shared with us, including requests to access, correct, or delete it, email zhekov.yane123@gmail.com.

04 / Questions and changes

Questions about this page can be sent to zhekov.yane123@gmail.com. This page will be updated if the website's use of forms, analytics, or other information-handling features changes.
